Who we are

Dance is our language

We are a Kenyan dance crew brought together by rhythm, creativity and the joy of moving as one. From our first steps to the stages ahead, we keep learning, creating and showing up with purpose.

Our purpose is to bring people together through performance and make dance welcoming to everyone who wants to learn.
